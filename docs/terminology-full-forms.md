Written 2026-10-04. She has a **PhD in Earth Sciences** and holds certifications in **GRI, SBTi,
CDP and Integrated Reporting**. In front of her, two things cost you credibility: getting an
expansion wrong, and expanding something that is no longer expanded. **Read the trap box first.**

---

## ⚠️ READ THIS FIRST — three names you must NOT spell out

These are the traps, and they are exactly the ones a newcomer walks into.

### 1. **CDP** — do not say "Carbon Disclosure Project"

It was originally the Carbon Disclosure Project. **It officially dropped that name in 2013**,
because it had expanded beyond carbon into water and forests. The organisation is now called
**CDP, full stop.**

Saying "Carbon Disclosure Project" instantly marks you as someone who read an old article.
**Say "CDP."** If you want to show you know the history: *"CDP — it used to be the Carbon
Disclosure Project, before it went beyond carbon."* That sentence earns you credit. Using the old
name as if current loses it.

### 2. **EcoVadis** — there is NO expansion. It is a company name.

**EcoVadis is not an acronym.** It is the name of a French company, founded 2007, that sells
supplier sustainability ratings. There is nothing to spell out.

**If you try to expand it, you will invent something, and she will notice.** Say "EcoVadis."
Note the capitalisation: **E**co**V**adis.

### 3. **GRESB** — the old full form is now actively wrong

It began as the **Global Real Estate Sustainability Benchmark**. It then expanded to cover
**infrastructure as well as real estate** — so the organisation now goes by **GRESB** alone,
because "Real Estate" no longer describes its full scope.

**Say "GRESB."** If it comes up: *"GRESB — it started as the Global Real Estate Sustainability
Benchmark, but it covers infrastructure too now, which is why they just use the acronym."*

**Also: MSCI** no longer expands either. It was Morgan Stanley Capital International; it is now
just MSCI. Don't expand it.

**How they're commonly said aloud:** GRESB as one word, roughly *"GREZ-bee."* EcoVadis,
*"EE-koh-VAH-dis."* These are conventions, not rules — but mispronouncing a framework's name in
front of its certified practitioner is an avoidable cost.

## The four in your email

| Acronym | Full form | One line |
|---|---|---|
| **BRSR** | **Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report** | SEBI's mandatory filing for the top 1,000 listed Indian companies. SEBI's own circulars say "Report"; "Reporting" is used loosely for the regime. |
| **CDP** | ⚠️ **None — formerly Carbon Disclosure Project, renamed 2013** | Global environmental disclosure platform. Companies respond because an investor or a big customer asked. Scored A to D−. |
| **EcoVadis** | ⚠️ **None — it is a company name** | Supplier sustainability ratings. A buyer tells its suppliers to get rated. Scored 0–100 with a medal. Rates **management systems**: policy, actions, results. |
| **GRESB** | ⚠️ **None now — formerly Global Real Estate Sustainability Benchmark** | Benchmark for real-estate and infrastructure entities and funds. Scored out of 100 and ranked against peers. |

## Emissions and measurement — the words in your own calculators

| Term | Full form / meaning |
|---|---|
| **GHG** | **Greenhouse gas** |
| **GHG Protocol** | **Greenhouse Gas Protocol** — the global accounting standard, from **WRI** (World Resources Institute) and **WBCSD** (World Business Council for Sustainable Development) |
| **Scope 1** | Emissions from sources the company **owns or controls** — fuel burnt on site, company vehicles, refrigerant leaks |
| **Scope 2** | Emissions from **energy the company buys** — in practice, purchased electricity |
| **Scope 3** | Emissions in the **value chain** — travel, commuting, freight, waste, purchased goods |
| **tCO₂e** | **Tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent** — all gases converted to a CO₂-equivalent basis |
| **GWP** | **Global Warming Potential** — how much more warming a gas causes than CO₂ over 100 years. Refrigerants are reported this way. |
| **IPCC** | **Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change** — source of your fuel factors |
| **AR5** | The IPCC's **Fifth Assessment Report** — where your GWP values come from |
| **NCV** | **Net Calorific Value** — energy per unit of fuel; how you get from litres to joules |
| **DEFRA** | **Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs** (UK) — ⚠️ the GHG conversion factors moved to **DESNZ, the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero**, in 2023. Your data file cites **DEFRA/DESNZ** for that reason. |
| **RF** | **Radiative forcing** — the extra warming from emitting at altitude; included in air-travel factors |
| **Location-based vs market-based Scope 2** | Location-based uses the grid average where you are; market-based uses contracts you hold. ⚠️ **CDP asks for both; BRSR asks for one.** A good thing to know. |
| **HSD / MS / CNG / LPG / HFO** | **High Speed Diesel / Motor Spirit (petrol) / Compressed Natural Gas / Liquefied Petroleum Gas / Heavy Fuel Oil** |
| **SF₆** | **Sulphur hexafluoride** — switchgear insulating gas, extremely high GWP |
| **NOx / SOx / PM** | **Oxides of nitrogen / oxides of sulphur / particulate matter** |

## The honest fallback — use it rather than guess

An acronym will come up that isn't on this sheet. **Say so.**

> "I don't know that one — what does it stand for?"

**Asking is free. Guessing in front of a PhD is not.** And someone who trains practitioners for a
living will read the question as engagement, not ignorance — teaching is literally what she does.

⚠️ **The one unbreakable rule: never expand an acronym you are not sure of.** An invented
expansion is the single fastest way to tell an expert you don't know the field.
