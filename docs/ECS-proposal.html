Who I am

I build Saaksh, a small set of tools for BRSR and ESG work in India, shaped with input from practising consultants including Priya Ranjan. I can also work hands-on as a software and BRSR consultant. I am a solo founder, so anything we agree is quick to try and easy to stop.

Where Saaksh could fit into your BRSR process

Your published BRSR process What Saaksh could contribute
1 · Assessment & Strategy “Gap analysis against BRSR requirements” A free readiness check that gap-analyses a company across all 108 Section‑C disclosures in about two minutes, cited to SEBI and ICAI — a first pass before your team’s detailed assessment, and something ECS can offer prospects at no cost.
2 · Data Collection & Management “Data collection across ESG metrics” Collect sends branded requests to each data owner at the client, chases them with automatic reminders, and shows you who has submitted and who has not — with emissions computed and a full attribution trail behind every figure.
3 · Report Development “BRSR section drafting” A draft generated from the collected figures, with every number traceable to the person who submitted it. Your team reviews and signs off — nothing is ever auto-trusted.
4 · Final Reporting & Integration The same attribution trail is what an assurance provider asks for — already assembled, rather than reconstructed at the end.
On Redefine — where I do and don’t fit. Redefine lists automated ESG data gathering among its capabilities, so let me be straightforward: I am not proposing to replace it. Saaksh is additive before the platform — a free, no-login check a prospect can use without becoming a client — and on engagements where standing a platform up for one cycle is more effort than the work warrants. If Redefine already covers collection well for you, options 1 and 3 still stand on their own. I would rather find the honest boundary with you than assume one.

What I have verified so far

  • Accuracy, tested against a real filing — on a limited-assured FY 2025‑26 BRSR, Saaksh reproduced the assured energy and water figures exactly, and Scope 2 to within 0.1%.
  • The regulatory skeleton is source-checked — all 108 Section‑C disclosures reconciled against the SEBI format and ICAI background material, including SEBI’s 2025 Green Credits amendment.
  • It is already live — the free tool, calculators and guides are in public use at saaksh.co today, so there is nothing to build before we try this.
