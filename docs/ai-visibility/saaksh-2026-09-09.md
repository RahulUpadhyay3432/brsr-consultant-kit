## Coverage and method — read this before trusting a number

Measured by running natural-language buyer questions through live web search and reading the
AI-synthesised answer plus its cited source list. **ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Grok were not
queried directly** — no API or logged-in session was reachable from this run. What is measured
here is the answer-with-citations surface over a live search index, which is a fair proxy for
Google AI Overviews and for any assistant answering with search grounding, and is *not* the same
as ChatGPT's own answer. Treat platform-specific claims as unmeasured.

Search locale is US-default, so India-local ranking may differ. 9 queries, 1 surface. Small sample:
directional, not a score to defend to a client.

## Priority 2 — what the push does not fix

**Correction to an earlier read in this run: the crosswalk page already exists.**
`/tools/brsr-framework-mapping` renders the full merged crosswalk (BRSR/GRI/TCFD/IFRS S1-S2/TNFD/ESRS),
carries its own FAQ schema, offers CSV and Word export, and is already in the sitemap at priority
0.85. It was added in commit `c6506cc` and is unpushed, which is the only reason it did not appear.
It belongs in Priority 1 above, not here. No new page is needed — and building one would have
duplicated it and split the canonical.

This makes the deploy gap the whole story for query #8: an AI told a user outright that it could not
find a downloadable BRSR crosswalk, on a question where we have the best asset on the open web,
finished and sitting in a local commit.

**BRSR Core assurance (query #4)** is a content-quality gap, not an absence. `/tools/audit-readiness`
is live and is exactly the per-KPI source-document list auditors demand, but it did not surface
against nine competitors with dedicated assurance pages. Its title is "BRSR Core Audit Readiness
Checklist" — it should carry the question shape people actually ask ("what evidence does a BRSR Core
assurance provider ask for?"), a 40–55 word direct answer near the top, and the per-KPI table
rendered as text rather than only behind an interactive filter.

**The directory (query #9)** cannot compete while empty. Upwork and Guru own the query. This does
not change the plan — seed by hand — it just confirms the board is worth nothing until it has rows.

## GEO recommendations, ranked by measured effect

From the Princeton GEO study effect sizes. Apply the top three per page, not all nine.

1. **Cite authoritative sources (+40%).** Already a house habit — every figure carries publication,
   section, version and URL. This is Saaksh's structural advantage over the competitor pages, which
   assert numbers without provenance. Keep leading with it.
2. **Add statistics and specific numbers (+37%).** `/brsr/statistics` was built precisely for this
   and is unpushed. Once live it is the single most quotable asset on the site.
3. **Authoritative tone, no hedging (+25%).** The fees post's refusal to invent a benchmark is
   correct and must stay — but state the *method* with authority rather than hedging the whole page.
4. **Never keyword-stuff (−10%, actively harmful).** No action; the site does not do this.

Block sizing to apply: 134–167-word self-contained passages for AI-answer citation, and a 40–55
word direct answer near the top of each page for featured-snippet/PAA capture. Every FAQ answer
added in the backfill should be checked against the 40–55 word shape.
