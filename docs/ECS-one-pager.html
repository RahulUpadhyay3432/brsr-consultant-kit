Making BRSR delivery faster, and winning more BRSR clients
A proposal from Rahul Upadhyay, founder of Saaksh, to explore how Saaksh can complement ECS’s BRSR practice and the Redefine platform.

Who I am

I build Saaksh, a set of tools for BRSR and ESG work in India, shaped with input from practising consultants including Priya Ranjan. I know BRSR well and can also work hands-on as a software and BRSR consultant.

What Saaksh does today (live)

  • Free BRSR readiness tool — describe a client in about 2 minutes and get a gap-analysis across all 108 Section-C disclosures, cited to SEBI & ICAI, with built-in GHG, energy and water calculators. Runs on-device, no login.
  • Collect (paid) — chase BRSR data from a client’s teams with branded requests and reminders, auto-compute emissions with a full attribution and assurance trail, and generate a draft.
  • Accuracy, validated — tested against a real limited-assured FY25-26 BRSR: reproduced the assured energy & water figures exactly, and Scope 2 within 0.1%.
How this complements Redefine, rather than competes. Saaksh is a free, instant, India-BRSR-specialised front door. Redefine is your platform for engaged clients. Saaksh sits above it in the funnel (capturing leads) and beside it in delivery (speeding the BRSR groundwork), with zero build cost to ECS.
