## Firm tier — what shipped 2026-09-30

Collect was single-tenant in a way that mattered: `listCampaigns()` ran
`select * from brsr_requests` with **no WHERE clause**, `Campaign` had no owner, and one shared
`CONSULTANT_PASSCODE` let in everyone — so any consultant who signed in saw **every** other
consultant's clients, contacts, values and evidence. That ruled out selling to a firm.

Now: a firm (org) owns its campaigns, and `createCampaign`, `listCampaigns`, `getCampaign`,
`updateCampaign`, `deleteCampaign` and `setNarrative` are all filtered by `org_id`.

- `CONSULTANT_PASSCODES` (env, `lib/datarequest/passcodes.ts`) is the edge-runtime fast path
  middleware checks — still a real comparison, still fails closed, just against every firm's
  passcode rather than one.
- `brsr_orgs` (table) carries the `org_id` the queries filter on. **That is what isolates data.**
- **Adding a firm needs BOTH.** Env-only fails closed (`/login?error=setup`) rather than
  falling through and showing that firm everyone else's clients.
- The default firm (`slug='saaksh'`) has **no passcode in the database** — the original
  `CONSULTANT_PASSCODE` stays in the environment and resolves that firm **by slug**, so a live
  secret is never copied into a table.
- `requireConsultant()` — already inside all 17 sensitive server actions, because server actions
  can be POSTed to any route — now returns the resolved firm instead of `void`.

**Known gap, not closed:** contact/item-level writes (`updateItem`, `setItemEvidence`,
`setContactStatus`) are still only passcode-gated and would accept another firm's id. Reaching
one needs an unguessable uuid no scoped listing hands out. **If she presses on data separation:**
*"firm-level separation on collections is in; the row-level hardening is the next commit."*

**Also unbuilt and next:** per-person seats inside a firm (Supabase Auth + invites +
attribution). Today SAGE's 17 people would share **one passcode** and all see all SAGE clients.

## Build 2 — field-level CDP / EcoVadis for Principle 6 (shipped 2026-09-30)

Makes "collect once, report across frameworks" concrete for the disclosures clients are asked
for repeatedly: energy, water, GHG, waste, biodiversity.

`src/data/cdp_ecovadis_mappings.json` follows the **existing overlay pattern** (`tnfd_mappings.json`,
`esrs_mappings.json`): a sparse file keyed by `framework_mappings.json`'s `brsr_id`, merged into
`FrameworkMapping` as optional fields in `report-generator.ts`, and rendered as two more entries
in `FrameworkMapper`'s framework registry.

**It invents no vocabulary.** Every `cdp_area` and `ecovadis_criterion` is drawn verbatim from the
already-sourced principle-level sets in `esg_ratings_mapping.json` (9 CDP areas reflecting the
2024-onward integrated questionnaire; EcoVadis' documented 21 criteria). `cdp-ecovadis-mappings.test.ts`
asserts that, plus that every key resolves against the crosswalk and that the **sparseness survives**:
waste rows carry no CDP area (CDP has no waste module), scenario analysis and internal carbon pricing
carry no EcoVadis criterion (EcoVadis assesses management systems), and `P6-E24`
(non-compliances/penalties) claims no mapping at all.

⚠️ **Two `brsr_id` conventions exist.** `framework_mappings.json` is finer-grained than
`brsr_data_points.json` — it splits multi-part disclosures into separate rows, so P6 has **27** rows
there versus 13 essential + 8 leadership indicators in the data-points file. Overlays key off the
**crosswalk** ids. Getting this wrong produces mappings that silently resolve to nothing.
